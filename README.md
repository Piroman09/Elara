# 🎵 ELARA

### Your music. Your vibe. ✨

ELARA is a modern and aesthetic Android music player built for people who simply want to **listen to music and enjoy the experience**.

No complicated setup. Just download, install, and start listening. 🎧

## ✨ What you can do with ELARA

🎵 **Listen to your music**  
Enjoy your favorite tracks with a clean and simple player.

🔍 **Find your music**  
Search through songs and artists easily.

▶️ **Powerful music controls**  
Play, pause, skip, rewind, and control your music effortlessly.

🎨 **Beautiful interface**  
A minimal and aesthetic design made for an enjoyable listening experience.

🎧 **Keep the music playing**  
Enjoy your music while using other parts of your phone.

📖 **Lyrics**  
View lyrics while listening to supported songs.
